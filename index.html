Dr Kellin Publisher

Dr Kellin Publisher is a private desktop tool used by the creator of the TikTok account @drkellin.med to upload their own finished educational animations (Dr. Kellin, a fictional medical student, PSX-style 3D animation) to that account through TikTok's official Content Posting API.

It is not offered to other users. It only posts videos that the account owner made, reviewed and chose to publish, with TikTok's AI-generated content label turned on.
