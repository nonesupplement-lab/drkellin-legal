Privacy Policy — Dr Kellin Publisher

Last updated: 28 October 2026

  • Whose data. Only the data of the single account owner (@drkellin.med) who authorizes the tool. It is not used by or offered to anyone else, and it does not collect data about viewers or other TikTok users.
  • What is used. The OAuth access/refresh token and open ID that TikTok returns when the owner authorizes the app, the creator information TikTok returns for posting (such as allowed privacy options), and the owner's own video files and captions.
  • Why. Only to upload the owner's videos to the owner's account and check their publishing status.
  • Storage. Tokens are stored only on the owner's own computer, in the operating system's protected storage, never in source code or shared files. Nothing is sold or shared with third parties.
  • Deletion. The owner can revoke access at any time in TikTok's settings (Security → Manage app permissions); local tokens are then deleted.
  • Contact. Through the TikTok account @drkellin.med.
